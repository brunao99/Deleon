FAQ

Questions before you sit down.

Didn't find your answer? Give the shop a call.

How do I book?

Call us at (415) 368-2421. We'll find a time that works for you and hold the chair.

Do you take walk-ins?

If a chair is open, come on in. Calling ahead is the surest way to skip the wait.

What services do you offer?

Hair cuts and fades, beard trims and head shaves. Have something specific in mind? Ask when you call.

Can I bring a reference photo?

Please do. A photo of the cut you want is the fastest way to get it right the first time.

Where are you located?

2138 Mission St, San Francisco, CA 94110, in the Mission District, a short walk from 16th St Mission BART. Look for the black storefront with the red trim and the "Barbershop" sign.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday from 10 AM to 9 PM, and Sunday from 10 AM to 3 PM. Call ahead and we'll tell you when the next chair is free.
